## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for grassroots civic management in Indonesian neighborhoods (Rukun Tetangga / RT). It balances the administrative precision required for resident data, dues collection (iuran), and letter issuance with a warm, communal character that removes the cold intimidation typical of government bureaucracy.

### Brand Personality & Tone
- **Amanah & Tertib (Trustworthy & Organized):** Clean governance, dependable data protection, and structural clarity for RT/RW officers.
- **Guyub & Hangat (Communal & Welcoming):** Warm accents and friendly curves that evoke an approachable neighborhood mutual-cooperation (*gotong royong*) atmosphere.
- **Transparan & Jelas (Transparent & Legible):** Information-dense dashboards with high legibility for residents of all ages, from digital-native youth to senior neighborhood heads (*Ketua RT*).

### Design Style: Modern Civic Dashboard
A hybrid of clean modern SaaS and tactile warmth. Surfaces combine crisp white cards with soft warm-cream underlays, friendly 12px to 16px border radiuses, understated ambient shadows tinted with soft teal, and vibrant coral highlights for urgent alerts and high-priority resident actions.

## Colors

The color palette reinforces civic trust, readability, and neighborhood vitality while avoiding institutional gloom.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#359FA0` - Deep Teal):** The core brand color representing accountability, governance, and quiet authority. Used for active navigation tabs, primary buttons, table header borders, and key data visualization markers.
- **Secondary (`#8AD6D1` - Soft Aqua / Mint):** Used for supporting elements, hover states on primary surfaces, badge fills for completed statuses, and subtle metric chart fills.
- **Tertiary / Action Accent (`#FF8C52` - Warm Coral):** Designates call-to-action triggers, emergency/panic button triggers (*Tombol Darurat*), overdue payment notifications, and priority badges.
- **Surface / Ambient Base (`#FFF0C5` - Warm Cream):** Applied strategically to contextual community hero sections, banner highlights, announcement ribbons, and warm subtle background washes to introduce neighborhood warmth.
- **Neutrals:**
  - `Neutral Dark (#1F2937 - Slate Gray)`: Dominant text and icon color to avoid the harsh contrast of pitch black.
  - `Neutral Muted (#6B7280)`: Secondary meta-labels, timestamps, and column headers.
  - `Neutral Border (#E5E7EB)`: Delimiting lines for tables and inputs.
  - `Neutral Light (#F9FAFB)`: Canvas background for desktop administration panels.
  - `Pure White (#FFFFFF)`: Elevated data cards, modals, and input fields.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is selected across all roles to provide geometric precision coupled with humanist legibility. Its open counters and modern Indonesian design heritage align naturally with civic systems.

### Usage Rules
- **Display & Headlines:** Used for dashboard greeting headlines (e.g., "Selamat Datang, Pengurus RT 04"), civic statistical totals, and critical module titles.
- **Body & Captions:** Tuned for maximum data legibility within dense citizen tables (*Daftar Kepala Keluarga*), identity number displays (*NIK/No KK*), and announcement text.
- **Numerics:** Use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) across citizen counts, rupiah currency metrics (`Rp 12.500.000`), and dates to preserve vertical column alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an administrative fluid-responsive grid system designed for desktop officer terminals, tablet-based field surveys, and resident mobile screens.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout with a fixed 260px collapsible sidebar for navigation. Main canvas runs on `gutter: 1.5rem` and outer page margins of `2rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with rail navigation or off-canvas drawer. Outer margins adjust to `1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with bottom app navigation. Gutters drop to `1rem` and canvas margins to `1rem`.

### Spacing Rhythm
- **Micro Spacing (`space-xs`, `space-sm`):** Reserved for badge paddings, input group icon offsets, and tabular cell row heights.
- **Component Spacing (`space-md`, `space-lg`):** Standard interior padding for metric stat cards, form field vertical stacks, and community announcement blocks.
- **Macro Spacing (`space-xl`):** Inter-card margins, table-to-metric separators, and container section divides.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through subtle ambient shadowing with a cool-tinted baseline rather than muddy grays, keeping the software airy and clean.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Background surfaces (`#F9FAFB` or light warm washes `#FFF0C5` at 25% opacity) without shadows.
- **Level 1 (Data Cards & Tables):** Elevated with a subtle ambient teal-slate drop shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.05);` and a hairline border `1px solid #E5E7EB`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Interactive Containers):** Raised cards on hover and stat widgets:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(53, 159, 160, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.05);`.
- **Level 3 (Dropdowns & Popovers):** Contextual resident action menus:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.03);`.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Emergency Alerts):** Centered dialogs, citizen verification overlays:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(31, 41, 55, 0.08);`.

## Shapes

The design uses a friendly, approachable corner radius (Scale 2 - Rounded) reflecting community warmth without compromising administrative density.

- **Inputs, Buttons, and Badges (`rounded-md` / `0.5rem` to `0.75rem`):** Confident curves that soften technical entry fields.
- **Cards, Modals, and Flyouts (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl` / `1rem` to `1.5rem`):** Generous, inviting containment for citizen summaries and metric widgets.
- **Pills / Status Chips (`rounded-full`):** Used strictly for citizen tags (e.g., "Warga Tetap", "Warga Kontrak", "Lunas", "Tertunda").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Teal (`#359FA0`) solid fill with white text, font weight 600, padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`, radius `0.75rem`. Hover state transitions to `#2C8586`.
- **Secondary:** Soft Aqua tint (`#8AD6D1` at 20% opacity) with `#1F2937` or `#359FA0` text for auxiliary actions like export to Excel or view details.
- **Accent / Alert (CTA):** Warm Coral (`#FF8C52`) fill with white text for urgent community alerts, overdue balance billing prompts, and high-impact confirmations.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with `#359FA0` text and soft hover fill (`#F9FAFB`).

### Cards & Metrics Widgets
- Encased in `#FFFFFF` with a crisp 1px border (`#E5E7EB`) and Level 1 elevation.
- Metric cards display numerical totals in tabular bold (`headline-lg`), a compact trend indicator badge, and a contextual micro-icon container tinted with `#8AD6D1` or `#FFF0C5`.

### Data Tables (Warga & Iuran Registry)
- Header row styled with `#F9FAFB` background, uppercase `label-sm` slate text, and subtle bottom border.
- Row items separated by hairline `1px solid #F3F4F6` borders with hover highlight `rgba(138, 214, 209, 0.08)`.
- Alternating or highlighted rows for priority resident verification statuses.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Height `44px` on desktop with `0.75rem` radius, 1px border (`#D1D5DB`), text `#1F2937`, and placeholder `#9CA3AF`.
- Focus state features a crisp outline in `#359FA0` with a 3px ring of `#8AD6D1` (opacity 30%).

### Status Chips & Badges
- Pill shape (`rounded-full`) with `label-sm` typography and interior padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- **Lunas (Paid / Verified):** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, dot indicator `#10B981`.
- **Belum Bayar (Pending):** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, dot indicator `#F59E0B`.
- **Darurat / Peringatan (Emergency):** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, dot indicator `#FF8C52`.

### Civic Specific: Announcement Banner & Panic Module
- **Warga Notice Card:** Warm Cream background (`#FFF0C5` at 40% fill) with `#1F2937` typography and Deep Teal icon framing for agenda notices (*Kerja Bakti*, *Ronda Malam*).
- **Tombol Darurat (Panic Button):** Vibrant Warm Coral button with pulsing radial ring for instant neighborhood watch security calls.
